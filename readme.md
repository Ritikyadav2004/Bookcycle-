# BookCycle 📚

> *Empowering communities by making second-hand book trading accessible to everyone.*

## About The Project
BookCycle is a platform designed to connect people who want to buy and sell pre-owned books. Our mission is to promote sustainable reading habits and make educational and recreational literature more affordable for everyone.

## Status
🚧 **This project is currently under construction.** Features and updates are being actively added.

## Objective
To provide a reliable, user-friendly marketplace where individuals can easily buy older books or give their read books a new home.
